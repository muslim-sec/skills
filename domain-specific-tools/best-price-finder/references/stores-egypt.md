# Egypt — store reference

Currency: EGP (ج.م). VAT is typically included in displayed prices on consumer sites.

## Mega-marketplaces (always check)
- **Amazon.eg** — amazon.eg — broad catalog, decent return policy
- **Noon.com** — noon.com (Egypt store) — competitive pricing, frequent flash sales
- **Jumia Egypt** — jumia.com.eg — broad catalog but seller quality varies, check seller rating
- **Yaoota** — yaoota.com — price comparison aggregator; useful as a cross-reference

## Traps to watch for
- **Jumia third-party sellers with <4★ rating** — frequent fakes/wrong-item issues
- **Facebook Marketplace prices in your search results** — exclude unless user asks; can't verify stock or seller
- **OLX listings** — same as above, used market not retail
- **Gray imports** ("استيراد") on Amazon.eg / Noon — often legit but no manufacturer warranty in Egypt; flag this
