# UAE — store reference

Currency: AED (د.إ). VAT 5% — usually included in displayed prices.

## Mega-marketplaces (always check)
- **Amazon.ae** — amazon.ae — broad catalog, Prime available
- **Noon.com (UAE)** — noon.com — competitive, regular sales (Yellow Friday, White Friday)

- **Eros Group** — eros.ae
- **Apple UAE** — apple.com/ae

## Traps to watch for
- **Dubai Mall / Mall of the Emirates physical-only items** — sometimes priced higher than online; worth comparing
- **Gray imports on Amazon.ae** — common for cameras, watches; no UAE warranty
- **Dubizzle** — used market, not retail; exclude unless user asks
