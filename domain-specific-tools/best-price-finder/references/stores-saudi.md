# Saudi Arabia — store reference

Currency: SAR (ر.س). VAT 15% — usually included in displayed prices for consumer goods.

## Mega-marketplaces (always check)
- **Amazon.sa** — amazon.sa — broad catalog, Prime available
- **Noon.com (KSA)** — noon.com — competitive, regular sales (Yellow Friday)
- **AliExpress KSA** — aliexpress.com — cheap imports, long delivery, no local warranty

## Electronics & tech specialists
- **Jarir Bookstore** — jarir.com — electronics, books, office; strong physical chain
- **Extra (eXtra)** — extrastores.com — major electronics retailer, online + physical
- **AlMana / AX** — local electronics chains
- **Saco** — saco.sa — home improvement, tools, some electronics
- **Apple Saudi Arabia** — apple.com/sa
- **Samsung KSA** — samsung.com/sa

## Traps to watch for
- **AliExpress / DHgate** prices look amazing but no local warranty, customs fees can apply
- **Re-imported phones** on Noon/Amazon — sometimes missing Arabic keyboards or local certifications
- **Unofficial Apple resellers** — for AppleCare in KSA you usually need to buy from authorized channels
