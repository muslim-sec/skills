# LinkedIn Profile Sections — Detailed Reference

This file contains detailed specs, limits, and coaching notes for every LinkedIn profile section.

---

## 1. NAME

- **Fields**: First Name, Last Name
- **Tips**:
  - Use your real, professional name (or the name you're known by professionally)
  - Avoid adding titles like "Dr." or "CEO" in the name field — those go in the headline
  - If you have a common name, consistency across platforms helps searchability
  - For Arabic profiles: add the profile in Arabic via "Add profile in another language"

---

## 2. PROFESSIONAL PHOTO

- **Recommended size**: 400 × 400 pixels (minimum 200×200, max 8MB)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Best practices**:
  - Face fills 60% of the frame
  - Look directly at the camera
  - Professional attire appropriate to your industry
  - Solid color or softly blurred background
  - Good, even lighting (natural light or ring light)
  - Genuine smile — approachable yet confident
- **What to avoid**:
  - Casual/party photos
  - Sunglasses
  - Group photos cropped awkwardly
  - Outdated photos (use something from the last 2-3 years)
- **Analysis dimensions** (when user uploads):
  - Confidence (1-10)
  - Professionalism (1-10)
  - Likeability (1-10)
  - Influence (1-10)

---

## 3. COVER PHOTO (BACKGROUND BANNER)

- **Size**: 1584 × 396 pixels (recommended)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG, max 8MB
- **Purpose**: Visual brand statement — this is prime real estate
- **What to put here**:
  - Your name + tagline/headline
  - Your logo or personal brand colors
  - A CTA ("Book a free call → link")
  - Industry-relevant visual or your workspace/event photo
  - Social proof ("Helped 500+ professionals land their dream jobs")
- **Free template source**: https://www.canva.com/linkedin-banners/templates/
- **Tips**: Keep text minimal and readable on mobile. Use brand-consistent colors.

---

## 4. HEADLINE

- **Character limit**: 220 characters (show ~120-180 for best display across devices)
- **Visibility**: Appears everywhere — search results, connection requests, comments, DMs
- **Formula**:
  > [Role/Title] | [Who you help] + [How/Result] | [Keywords/Credentials]
- **Elements to include**:
  - Core value proposition
  - Target audience (who you help)
  - Searchable keywords (what recruiters/clients type)
  - Key credentials or differentiators
- **Examples by goal**:

  *Job seeker*:
  > Software Engineer | React & Node.js | Building scalable web apps | Open to Senior Dev roles

  *Entrepreneur/Consultant*:
  > Business Growth Consultant | Helping SMEs Scale Revenue 3x | Strategy • Sales • Digital Marketing

  *Arabic (bilingual)*:
  > مستشار تسويق رقمي | أساعد العلامات التجارية على النمو في السوق السعودي | Digital Marketing | KSA & Gulf

- **Generate 3 options** for the user to choose from or mix.

---

## 5. CURRENT POSITION

- If the user has **multiple current positions**:
  - Keep the most senior or most relevant one as "primary"
  - The others can be listed but the primary one drives search placement
  - For freelancers/consultants: create a company page or list as "Self-employed"
- **Tips**: The current position title feeds into keyword matching — optimize it.

---

## 6. INDUSTRY

- LinkedIn uses this for job matching and search visibility
- Choose the **most specific** industry that matches your work (not the broadest)
- If between two: choose the one your clients/recruiters most likely filter by
- It's a dropdown — browse carefully, there are hundreds of options

---

## 7. ABOUT (SUMMARY)

- **Character limit**: 2,600 characters (aim for 1,800–2,400)
- **Key facts**:
  - Only first 2-3 lines show before "See more" — make the hook count
  - Reads on mobile: use short paragraphs and line breaks
  - Write in first person ("I help..." not "John helps...")
  - Include keywords naturally — this section is indexed for search

**Recommended structure**:
1. **Hook** — Bold opening that stops the scroll (question, bold claim, striking stat)
2. **The Problem** — What challenge does your audience face?
3. **Your Solution** — How do you specifically help?
4. **Your Story/Credibility** — Brief background, proof points, achievements
5. **What Working With You Looks Like** — Services, approach, process
6. **Social Proof** — Numbers, client names, awards, results
7. **CTA** — "DM me", "Visit [website]", "Book a free call at [link]"

**Skills in About section**:
- Add top 5 skills via the Skills section (displayed under About)
- These improve matching for LinkedIn's algorithm

---

## 8. SKILLS

- **Maximum**: 100 skills, but focus on quality over quantity
- **Top 5** are highlighted prominently — choose these carefully
- LinkedIn members can **endorse** your skills (social proof)
- Skills feed into **job matching** and **search discovery**
- Tips:
  - Include both hard skills (tools, languages, platforms) and soft skills (leadership, strategy)
  - Align with keywords in your headline and about section
  - Remove outdated/irrelevant skills that dilute your focus

---

## 9. CTA BUTTON (CONTACT INFO / WEBSITE)

- You can add up to 3 website links with custom labels
- **Button types**: Website, Blog, Portfolio, LinkedIn profile (other), Other
- **Best uses**:
  - Booking/scheduling page (Calendly, Cal.com)
  - Personal website or portfolio
  - Linktree / bio page with multiple links
  - Free resource / lead magnet

**Coaching prompt to ask the user**: "Where do you want people to go after visiting your profile? What action should they take?"

---

## 10. SERVICES

- **Available to**: Creator Mode users with 10+ connections
- **Service listings**: Up to 10 services from dropdown categories
- **Each service includes**:
  - Service name (from dropdown)
  - About description: up to 500 characters
  - Pricing: set a price range OR "Contact for pricing"
  - Media: add images, videos, or links (demos, past projects, case studies)
- **Key setting**: Enable "Free messages from non-connections" — this is crucial for inbound leads
- **Pro tip**: Ask 3+ past clients for service reviews — they appear publicly on your profile

**Coaching prompts**:
- What are your top 10 services?
- What results have you delivered for past clients? (Use for the About field)
- Do you have demos, portfolio items, or case study links?

---

## 11. EDUCATION

- **Add all** relevant education: university degrees, diplomas, professional certifications, bootcamps
- **Fields per entry**:
  - School name
  - Degree / Certification
  - Field of study
  - Start and end dates
  - Grade (optional)
  - **Activities and societies** — clubs, student orgs, leadership roles
  - **Description** — what you studied, projects, thesis, key learnings
  - **Skills** — add skills associated with this education (improves matching)
  - **Media** — photos, certificates, links to capstone projects, videos

**Coaching note**: Education entries with rich descriptions and skills are weighted more heavily in LinkedIn's algorithm. Don't leave them blank.

---

## 12. PROJECTS

- **Title**: Make it result-driven and specific
  - ❌ "Marketing Campaign"
  - ✅ "Grew Email List 300% in 90 Days for SaaS Startup"
- **Description**: What you did, how, and the measurable result
- **Skills**: Add skills you used on the project
- **Media**: Website URL, data analysis file, video, images with team
- **Collaborators**: Tag team members who worked on the project
- **Associated with**: Link to your employer/company for this project

---

## 13. JOBS / OPEN TO WORK

**If job seeking**:
- Enable "Open to Work" (visible to all OR recruiters only)
- Configure preferences:
  - Job titles (be specific — list 3-5 target titles)
  - Locations (cities, remote, hybrid preferences)
  - Employment type (full-time, contract, part-time)
  - Start date

**Leverage AI job matching** (LinkedIn Premium):
- LinkedIn shows a match score for jobs vs. your profile
- Review what skills or experience gaps you have
- Use this to guide upskilling priorities

**Job saving**: Save relevant jobs to track them. Review daily if actively searching.

**Coaching note**: Ask the user what titles they're targeting, their location flexibility, and whether they're open to relocation or remote-first roles.

## GENERAL LINKEDIN GROWTH TIPS

1. **Multi-language profile**: Set up in all target languages (English + Arabic + others)
2. **LinkedIn Premium**: Invest in it — profile views data + InMail + job insights are worth it
3. **Follow top voices** in your field + target companies
4. **Daily engagement**: Like + comment + repost with your insights (5-10 min/day minimum)
5. **Mobile notifications**: Enable so you respond to comments and DMs promptly
6. **AI tools for content**: ChatGPT, Claude for writing | Midjourney for images | Veo for video
7. **Always add a CTA** — especially your profile button
8. **Organization verification**: Verify your employer/university when possible (trust signal)
9. **Study great profiles**: [Hossamudin Hassan](https://linkedin.com/in/hossamudin) — example of a well-optimized profile
10. **Create a company page** if you have a business — links back to your personal profile for credibility
11. **Post content regularly** — it's the #1 driver of profile growth and inbound opportunities
    - Learn content strategy from Hossamudin Hassan (free online resources)
